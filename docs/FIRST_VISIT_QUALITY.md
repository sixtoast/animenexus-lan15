# First visit quality (Creative Sprint 45)

## Principle

Polish without an intro movie. **Do not** fire Rive + full 3D Lantern + every SFX + heavy cinematography at once.

## Sequence

1. **CSS glow** — soft light on the card (`phase` 0→1)
2. **Soft SFX** — optional `ui_tap` then quiet `seal` (opt-in sound engine)
3. **Copy resolve** — “frequency opens” → “desk is yours”
4. **Interface** — dismiss; mascot / 3D stay available in the app (not forced on intro)

## Reduced motion

Skip intermediate phases; short still card; auto-dismiss.
